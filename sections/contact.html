Why a 10% Weight Buffer?

Carriers physically reweigh packages at their facility. If the actual weight is higher than declared, they charge a reweigh surcharge — sometimes $50–$150 extra — billed directly to the shipper.

CPARS adds a 10% buffer to your declared weight when fetching rates. This means the price you see already accounts for minor weight discrepancies, protecting you from surprise charges.

If the carrier confirms the weight is exactly as declared, any difference is credited back to you.
